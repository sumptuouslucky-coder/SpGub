SpGub Safety Centre

Our community principles

Be curious without being cruel. Challenge ideas, not people's dignity. No threats, harassment, hate, impersonation, spam, scams, or sharing private information without permission.

Safety tools

Use Report on a post or account. Block someone to prevent future direct conversations. You can also send a report directly to the SpGub team by email.

For emergencies, contact local emergency services. Do not rely on SpGub as an emergency service.
