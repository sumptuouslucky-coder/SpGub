SpGub privacy notice
Help & Safety Center 🛟

A clear place to understand SpGub, protect your account, and get help.

🌱 Be kind, be real

Share ideas without harassment, threats, hate, impersonation, private information, or spam. Disagree with an idea without attacking the person.

🔐 Account & privacy basics

Guest play is stored on this device where supported. Signing in enables shared community features that use Firebase. Do not post passwords, financial details, private addresses, or information about another person without permission.

💬 What works today?

Daily reflection questions, solo games, local game drafts, and some daily progress work on this device. The daily question can be added to a community-post draft. Community publishing depends on sign-in and Firebase permissions. Private chat, live friend rooms, country/global rounds, and public creator-game approval are not active yet.

🧰 Send useful bug details

This creates an email draft; it does not send anything automatically. Review it before sending. The diagnostics include only app version, browser, language, and connection status—not your posts or password.

📄 Privacy & terms

Read what this prototype stores locally, what may be shared through Firebase, and the rules for using SpGub.

These are prototype notices, not legal advice. Before public launch, publish the policies at stable public URLs and confirm they meet the laws and platform rules that apply.

📩 Contact or suggest something

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and which screen you were using.
